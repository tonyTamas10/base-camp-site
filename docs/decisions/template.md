# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed

## Context

What problem are we solving? What constraints apply (people, budget, time,
existing code)? State facts, not the solution.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to maintain.

## Alternatives considered

Each option we rejected, with the reason.
